Established in 1990 after the signing of the Sister-State agreement by Governors
Tommy Thompson (Wisconsin) and Takeshi Numata (Chiba),
Wisconsin Chiba, Inc. (WCI) supports the sister-state relationship between the State
of Wisconsin and Prefecture of Chiba, Japan.
WCI engages in activities such as organizing and hosting annual cultural delegations in partnership with its Japanese counterpart,
the [Chiba-Wisconsin Association](https://chiba-wisconsin.net/e/).
WCI is a non-profit, tax-exempt, volunteer-run 501(c)(3) organization.

## Mission

The WCI mission statement is as follows:

> Wisconsin Chiba, Inc (WCI). is dedicated to strengthening the sister-state relationship between Wisconsin and Chiba, Japan. By engaging children and adults through cultural exchange, educational collaboration, and community partnerships, WCI endeavors to build bridges for mutual understanding that will benefit both regions for generations to come.
